# Immigrant Economic Mobility in the U.S.

## Objective
Analyze how origin, entry path, and professional trajectory impact income and quality of life.

## Dataset
Synthetic dataset simulating immigrant life conditions in the U.S.

## Tools
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Questions
- Does English level impact income?
- Does entry type affect economic success?
- How common is career mismatch?
- What affects stress and satisfaction?

## Insights
- Advanced English correlates with higher income
- Many immigrants experience career mismatch
- Long working hours increase stress but not satisfaction
